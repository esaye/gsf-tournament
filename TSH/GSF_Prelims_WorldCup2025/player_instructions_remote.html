📱Step-by-Step Score Submission

  1. Complete Your Game: Finish your Scrabble game and calculate final scores
  2. Access the System: Open the tournament URL on your phone or device
  3. Login: Select your name and enter your 4-digit PIN
  4. Enter Scores: Input both your score and your opponent's score
  5. Submit: Click "Submit Game Result" - the system stores your submission
  6. Wait for Verification: Your opponent will receive a notification to verify
  7. Confirmation: If scores match, the result becomes final automatically
  8. Next Game: System will show your next pairing when ready
