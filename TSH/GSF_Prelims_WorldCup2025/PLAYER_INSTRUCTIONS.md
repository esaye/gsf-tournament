# 🇬🇲 GSF National Preliminary Tournament
## **Players & Spectators - Live Tournament Access**

### 📱 **How to Follow the Tournament Live**

#### **Step 1: Connect to WiFi**
- Make sure your phone/tablet/computer is on the **same WiFi network** as the tournament computer

#### **Step 2: Open Your Browser**
- Open any web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, etc.)

#### **Step 3: Go to Tournament URL**
**Type this address:** 

When the tournament director starts the server, they will run:
```bash
python3 get_tournament_urls.py
```

This will show the **exact URL** to share with players, such as:
- `http://192.168.1.XXX:8090` (your local network IP)

#### **Step 4: Bookmark for Easy Access**
- Save the tournament page to your bookmarks
- The page will refresh automatically every 30 seconds
